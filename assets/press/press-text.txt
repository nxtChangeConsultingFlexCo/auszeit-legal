SHORT DESCRIPTION

Off Hours is an app for iPhone and Android from Graz, Austria, that holds the apps you
choose during the hours you set and adds a short cooling-off first. Users then decide on
purpose: take a break or make an exception. On iPhone, Off Hours uses Screen Time; on
Android, the accessibility service. The app needs no account, no server and no analytics;
all data stays on the device. Off Hours is free with one schedule for up to two apps; Off
Hours Plus extends it. Karl Maier, MA, built it as a one-person project without investors;
the provider is nxtChange Consulting FlexCo.


FOUNDER

Karl Maier, MA, Founder and developer of Off Hours · nxtChange Consulting FlexCo, Graz, Austria

Karl Maier, MA, a management consultant and app developer from Graz, Austria, released Off
Hours for iPhone and Android in early October 2026: a one-person project without investors,
through his company nxtChange Consulting FlexCo. The reason was personal.

“Often I just wanted to take a quick look at Instagram, and then two hours were gone.”
Karl Maier, MA · founder of Off Hours

Off Hours interrupts that reflex. During a time window of your choice, usually in the
evening, around the clock if you like, the apps you pick first show a short pause with a
countdown. Then you decide on purpose: take a break or make an exception. No account, no
server, no tracking.
