App blocker for iPhone and Android

Put the phone down in the evening, without a fight with your willpower.

Off Hours holds the apps that keep you up at night during the hours you set. When you open one of them, your phone first shows a short cooling-off. Then you decide on purpose: take a break, or make an exception. No ban, no tricks, just a moment to think.

How it works: on iPhone, Off Hours uses Screen Time in iOS. On Android, Off Hours uses the accessibility service, which you turn on once. From then on the cooling-off starts on time, even when Off Hours is closed.

iPhone

iOS 17 or later · uses Screen Time

  1. Set a time window, for example 21:00 to 07:00, on the days you choose.
  2. Pick the apps. Off Hours uses your iPhone's Screen Time for this.
  3. From then on iOS takes over: the cooling-off starts on time, even when Off Hours is closed.
  4. After an exception the app stays open for a few minutes, then the cooling-off applies again.
  5. Home Screen widget with the current status.

Android

Android 8.0 or later · uses the accessibility service

  1. Set a time window, for example 21:00 to 07:00, on the days you choose.
  2. Pick the apps, one by one or as a whole category, from the list of apps on your phone.
  3. Turn on the accessibility service once. From then on the cooling-off starts on time, even when Off Hours is closed.
  4. After an exception the app stays open for a few minutes, then the cooling-off applies again.
  5. Home screen widget with the current status.

Privacy

No account. No data. No ads.

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.

On iPhone

Off Hours stores the apps you choose only as encrypted identifiers from iOS. Only the shield receives an app's name, to display it. Off Hours does not see how long you use apps, and it records nothing.

On Android

Off Hours uses Android's accessibility service only to notice which app is in front, and then shows the cooling-off over it. It does not read screen content, does not see what you type and records nothing. Off Hours does not see how long you use apps.

Frequently asked questions

Is Off Hours the same as Downtime in Apple's Screen Time?

No. Apple's Downtime blocks apps during set hours. Off Hours builds on Screen Time and puts a cooling-off with a decision in front of the app instead of a ban: take a break, or make an exception. Off Hours is a separate app by nxtChange Consulting FlexCo, not by Apple.

Does Off Hours only work in the evening?

No. You set the time window yourself, for example 21:00 to 07:00, on the days you choose: evenings, work hours or all day. The free tier has one schedule; Off Hours Plus adds more, for example for weekends.

What does Off Hours need on Android, and why the accessibility service?

An Android phone with Android 8.0 or later and Android's accessibility service, which you turn on once in Settings. Android has no Screen Time interface for other apps, and without a device-admin or enterprise profile there is no other way to show a cooling-off over another app at a scheduled time.

Off Hours uses the accessibility service only to notice which app is in front, and then shows the cooling-off over it. It does not read screen content, does not see what you type and records nothing. Before the app sends you to the accessibility settings, it explains this in a dialog.

Does Off Hours see which apps I use?

No. No account, no server, no analytics: everything stays on your phone. On iPhone, Off Hours stores the apps you choose only as encrypted identifiers from iOS; only the shield receives an app's name, to display it. On Android, the accessibility service handles only the package name of the app in front and does not store it. Off Hours does not see how long you use apps, and it records nothing.

Can I get around the block?

Yes. Off Hours is an aid, not a safeguard against yourself. Any schedule can be switched off at any time. If you want it stricter, Off Hours Plus offers a cooling-off of 30 or 90 seconds or locks the apps until the schedule ends.

What does Off Hours cost?

Off Hours is free with one schedule for up to two apps, a 15-second cooling-off and a 5-minute exception. Off Hours Plus costs €9.99 a year or €3.99 a month (Austrian price; Apple and Google convert it for each country, the exact price is shown in the app). The subscription renews automatically until you cancel it.

Does Off Hours pause websites too?

No. Off Hours pauses apps, not websites. Whether notifications from a paused app appear is up to the system.

Which devices does Off Hours run on?

On iPhone with iOS 17 or later and on Android phones with Android 8.0 or later. Off Hours is available on the App Store and Google Play, in English and German.
